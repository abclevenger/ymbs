---

## How to use this registry

Every public proof statement must clearly fall into one of these categories:

1. **Work performed directly through YMBS** — contracted and delivered by YMBS
2. **Prior experience of an identified team member** — attributed to the person, not YMBS
3. **Combined team experience** — attributed to the team collectively
4. **A current YMBS service commitment** — an operating promise the team has approved
5. **A generalized or anonymized engagement example** — clearly labeled as representative

Never blur these categories. Do not imply that a prior employer's clients were YMBS clients.
